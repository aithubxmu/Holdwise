You are building "QuoteLah", a hackathon MVP for the SDC Hackathon 2026, Domain 2 "Orbyte Smart SME Challenge" (sponsor: Alpha Red Solutions). Build it from scratch in this Repl. Work in small milestones and keep the app runnable after each one.

## Product in one sentence
QuoteLah turns a messy WhatsApp price enquiry sent to a small Malaysian shop into an accurate, ready-to-send quotation in minutes, while a human with the right role always approves before anything reaches the customer, and every action is written to an activity log.

## The required journey (this is what judges score — it must work end to end)
1. TRIGGER: a customer message arrives (simulated inbox + POST /api/messages webhook-style endpoint).
2. CONNECTED DATA: pull the product catalog, prices, stock levels, customer record (type, zone) and delivery zones.
3. DECISION LOGIC: AI is used ONLY to extract line items from unstructured text. All pricing, discounts, delivery fees, stock checks and approval routing are deterministic rules in code. The AI must never produce prices or products that are not in the catalog.
4. HUMAN APPROVAL: staff reviews/edits the draft; consequential quotes require Owner approval.
5. VISIBLE OUTCOME: the quote is "sent" to a simulated WhatsApp outbox, stock is reserved, and every step is recorded in an activity log.

## Hard constraints
- ALL data is synthetic and created by us. No real people, phone numbers, companies or customer records. Use obviously fake phone numbers like +60 12-000 0001.
- No real WhatsApp API, no real payments, no e-invoicing / tax filing, no legal or financial advice. Prices are shown as final; tax is out of scope.
- Login/RBAC is simulated: a "Act as: Owner / Staff / Viewer" switcher in the header. No real auth.
- Never commit secrets. Any API key lives in Replit Secrets only. Add a .gitignore that excludes .env*, node_modules, build output.
- Add a LICENSE file with the full Apache License 2.0 text.
- The app must keep working with NO AI key: implement a rule-based fallback extractor (alias dictionary + regex for quantities/units) and use it automatically when the LLM call fails, times out (8s) or no key is configured. Show a small badge on each draft: "Parsed by: AI" or "Parsed by: rules (fallback)".

## Data model (Drizzle)
- users: id, name, role ('owner' | 'staff' | 'viewer')
- customers: id, name, phone, type ('walk_in' | 'regular' | 'contractor'), zone_id
- delivery_zones: id, name, fee_rm
- products: id, sku, name, aliases (text[]), unit, price_rm, stock_qty, reserved_qty, category, variant_group (nullable)
- messages: id, customer_id, body, received_at, status ('new' | 'drafted' | 'needs_review' | 'awaiting_approval' | 'sent' | 'rejected')
- quotes: id, message_id, customer_id, status, subtotal, discount_pct, discount_rm, delivery_rm, total, parsed_by ('ai' | 'rules'), created_at, sent_at, valid_until
- quote_items: id, quote_id, raw_text, product_id (nullable), qty, unit_price, line_total, confidence (0–1), flags (text[])
- activity_log: id, at, actor_user_id (nullable = system), action, entity, entity_id, details (jsonb with before/after)

## Business rules (put them in `lib/rules.ts`, pure functions, with unit tests)
- Matching: match extracted item text against product name + aliases (normalised, case-insensitive, simple fuzzy match). Confidence < 0.7 → flag `low_confidence`. No match → flag `unknown_item` (no price, human must pick). Multiple products in the same variant_group match → flag `ambiguous_variant` with the options listed.
- Stock: requested qty > (stock_qty − reserved_qty) → flag `insufficient_stock` and show available qty.
- Discount by customer type: walk_in 0%, regular 3%, contractor 5%.
- Delivery fee from the customer's zone, or from a zone mentioned in the message if the extractor finds one.
- Quote valid for 3 days.
- Approval routing: if total > RM 500, OR discount > 5%, OR staff manually overrode any unit price → status 'awaiting_approval' and only an Owner can approve. Otherwise Staff can send directly. Viewer can never edit or send.
- Any quote with an unresolved flag cannot be sent.
